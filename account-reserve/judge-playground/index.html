What this example actually proves

The unchanged Work SDK creates authenticated ciphertext, discovers it with synthetic PRF material, verifies the saved work and opens an editable copy. Preparation also checks the separate example account. Work recovery never calls account unlock. All keys and records stay in this page's memory. Resetting the simulated original app is not a real network outage.

A physical passkey, a fresh browser profile, another device and independent hosting require separate tests. The evidence below records the physical tests already performed and their limits.



THE TRADEOFF
A way back needs
to exist before you need it.
While the original app works, prepare one encrypted snapshot. Later, the reserve credential finds that snapshot in a separate client. Open the work, finish it and export your copy.
A correctly retained encrypted file can also work. ContinuityKit removes the file-finding step, in exchange for setup, available reserve storage and trust in another client.
One snapshot. No automatic sync. No promise of recovery after losing the passkey or the reserve infrastructure.
